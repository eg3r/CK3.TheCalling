# The Calling - Courtiers of Renown

A Crusader Kings III mod that lets you call talented people to your court and choose who joins it.

## Features

### Current
- **Call Maidens to Court**: A new decision for landed rulers, child rulers included. A week later, three maidens arrive at your court and you choose one to join it.
- **Real People First**: Unmarried women within your reach answer the call if they are willing. Your reach grows with your title, and willingness follows vanilla's "Invite to Court", made more generous for a ruler's call. Daughters of living rulers stay home. If too few answer, new maidens fill the places.
- **Choose Who You Call**: Pick age, birth, faith and culture right on the decision screen. Your choice is remembered.
- **Know Who You Choose**: Each maiden is described like a guest in the court window, with her best skill and her best congenital trait.
- **She Stays**: The maiden you choose won't leave your court for 10 years. Real women you pass over stay where they are.
- **Game Rules**: Cost, cooldown, real or created candidates, and the chance of a positive congenital trait (Guaranteed, Half Chance or Vanilla). Negative congenital traits never appear.
- **Fair Refund**: Your prestige comes back if you lose your land in the meantime or none of the maidens can come anymore.
- **Fully Localized**: English, French, German, Spanish, Russian, Polish, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese.

### Coming Soon
- **More Callings**: Special tutors, language teachers and other courtiers of renown

## Installation

### Steam Workshop (Recommended)
1. Subscribe to the mod on the Steam Workshop
2. Launch Crusader Kings III
3. The mod will be enabled automatically in the launcher
4. Start or load your game

### Manual Installation
1. Download the repository
2. Copy the `mod` folder to the following location and rename it to `TheCalling`:
   ```
   Documents\Paradox Interactive\Crusader Kings III\mod\
   ```
3. Copy `descriptor.mod` to the same `mod` folder and rename it to `TheCalling.mod`
4. Launch Crusader Kings III
5. Enable "The Calling - Courtiers of Renown" in the launcher's Mods tab
6. Start or load your game

On Windows, `deploy_mod.bat` performs steps 2 and 3 for you.

## How to Use

1. Open the **Decisions** tab and select **"Call Maidens to Court"**
2. Choose who you call and send out the call
3. A week later, choose the maiden who joins your court

The game rules are under **Game Rules → The Calling** when starting a new game.

## Compatibility

- **CK3 Version**: 1.20.*
- **Ironman**: Yes (achievements stay enabled, except with the 50-prestige cost, the 1-year cooldown or "Guaranteed" congenital traits)
- **Save Game Compatible**: Yes (game rules can only be set when starting a new game)
- **Other Mods**: Compatible with most mods; no vanilla files are overwritten
